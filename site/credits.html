Where the Name Comes From

The site GBAtemp once had a group of dedicated DS cheat makers. As the author became involved with the site, many of them became great friends and helped expand the focus to also include PSP cheats. "Temp" is derived from GBAtemp, while "AR" indicates support for the Action Replay code types. Together: TempAR, the angry cheat device.
